Publications

2026

SCAMMER4U: Benchmarking PII Leakage in Autonomous Web Agents

EMNLP 2026 Main · First author · with BITS Pilani and Lam Research

A pre-registered benchmark measuring whether web agents leak a user's personal information when manipulated by adversarial websites during routine tasks. It pairs 91 attacker-controlled environments with benign controls under an eight-axis taxonomy of manipulation tactics, and measures PII leakage and attack detection jointly across four frontier models.

arXiv

2026

GeoAgent: A Benchmark for Active Visual Geolocation

EMNLP 2026 Findings · First author · with IIT Bhubaneswar

An agentic scaffold that lets vision-language models navigate real Street View environments before committing to a location, instead of analysing static images. The benchmark covers 1,200 locations across 100 cities, with human baselines and ablations that reveal geographic bias between developed and developing regions.

2025

Guardians of Generation: Dynamic Inference-Time Copyright Shielding with Adaptive Guidance for AI Image Generation

ICCVW 2025 · Soham Roy, Abhishek Mishra, Shirish Karande, Murari Mandal

An inference-time framework that shields diffusion-based image generation from reproducing copyrighted content, without retraining the model.
